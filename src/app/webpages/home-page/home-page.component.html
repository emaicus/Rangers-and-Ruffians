10th Anniversary Edition

Rangers and Ruffians is a game about brave adventurers in search of gold and glory. As an adventurer, you will explore the wilderness, fight fearsome monsters, and delve into dark dungeons. This new edition aims to bring the rules back to the basics, focusing on the things that make the game fun and easy to play. Among other things, 10th Anniversary edition has the following changes:
  1. Simplified Combat Roll to hit is removed, offhand actions return, and called shots make their debut. Changes to combat should make the game feel fast, tactical, and epic!
  2. Re-focus on the Dungeon Magic items, called artifacts, bestow special abilities upon the adventurers who claim them. Most artifacts are found in the dungeon, forcing adventurers to overcome difficult challenges to grow in power.
  3. Simplified Resolution When an adventurer attempts to perform something difficult, the game now calls for 2d6 tests with standard result tiers. This makes resolution easier for the Poohbah and dovetails with the called shot and informal magic subsystems!
  4. So much more! Read the full rules document to see what else changed. From Death's Door Changes to Called Shots, less healing to simplified weapons, this edition gets back to that original core of Rangers and Ruffians!
